Auctioneer Simulator
Privacy Policy

Publisher: Steven Pounds. Effective date: November 6, 2026.

Game data

The game stores career progress, settings, earned Fair Tickets and the latest 500 committed auction events in its private local app storage. It has no developer-operated user account, advertising SDK, analytics service or developer-operated server. Device backups may include this data under your Apple settings.

Optional voice input

Microphone and speech permissions are optional. When you start a voice-assisted call, the app requests on-device English speech recognition. If on-device recognition is unavailable, voice input remains disabled and touch controls remain available. The app does not save audio recordings or upload voice to a developer server. Temporary recognized text is cleared with the voice session. Recognition proposes actions; you review them before confirmation.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and supplies purchase entitlements. The app checks verified entitlements to unlock purchased cosmetics. The publisher does not receive payment-card details through the app. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you reset the career or remove the app and its data. Settings → Reset local career erases career progress, tickets and the local event journal. Purchased cosmetics remain associated with your Apple Account and can be restored. Manage device backups through your Apple settings. Revoke microphone and speech permissions at any time through iOS Settings.

Contact and changes

Contact pounds1@gmail.com for privacy questions. Any changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
