Auctioneer Simulator
Privacy Policy

Publisher: Steven Pounds. Effective date: November 6, 2026.

Game data

The game stores career progress, settings, earned Fair Tickets and the latest 500 committed auction events in its private local app storage. It has no developer-operated user account or developer-operated server. Device backups may include this data under your Apple settings.

Advertising

The game is free with advertising served by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers and information about ad views and taps to provide, personalize and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. When the app asks for tracking permission, your choice controls personalized advertising; declining keeps ads non-personalized. The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns advertising off entirely.

Your voice runs the ring

Chanting is the game's main control. When a lot is live, the app uses the microphone and on-device English speech recognition to hear the prices and calls you say out loud. Recognition runs on this device; the app does not save audio recordings or upload voice to a developer server. Temporary recognized text is cleared when the lot ends. If you decline the permissions or on-device recognition is unavailable, every part of the game remains fully playable by touch and controller.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and supplies purchase entitlements. The app checks verified entitlements to unlock purchased cosmetics. The publisher does not receive payment-card details through the app. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you reset the career or remove the app and its data. Settings → Reset local career erases career progress, tickets and the local event journal. Purchased cosmetics remain associated with your Apple Account and can be restored. Manage device backups through your Apple settings. Revoke microphone and speech permissions at any time through iOS Settings.

Contact and changes

Contact pounds1@gmail.com for privacy questions. Any changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
