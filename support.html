How do I accept a bid?

Chant the asking price. When a paddle rises, call the price again out loud and the bid is theirs. By touch: tap an upright numbered paddle, select the value displayed in ASKING, then tap Confirm bid. A wave without a paddle is not a bid. If the ring changes, select the buyer again.

Why can't I close?

Real bids must be resolved first. Going once and Going twice each have a timer. A late paddle cancels closing. If no bid meets reserve, the gavel passes the lot instead of selling it.

The chant isn't hearing me

Chanting uses on-device English recognition. Play somewhere reasonably quiet, hold the phone about a foot away, and call prices as plain numbers - "fifty-five, fifty-five" - loud and rhythmic. Check speech and microphone permissions in iOS Settings. If on-device recognition is unavailable on your device, every call in the game also works by touch.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore Purchases in Outfit the ring or Settings with the Apple Account that purchased the Founder's Collection. An internet connection may be required. For billing or refunds, use Apple's purchase support.

Adjust the game

Settings offers longer paddle windows, protected paddle timing, sound and haptics. Timing changes apply when the next lot starts. Pause before putting the phone down; the game also pauses when it leaves the foreground.

Report a problem

Send your device model, iOS version, game version, the lot name and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send your Apple password, payment-card information or private recordings.
